# TinyOps — Site Structure & Sitemap
*Version 1.0 • Ratified June 2026*

This document outlines the informational architecture and page structure for the TinyOps web platform. Designed to be lightweight, fast, and optimized for high conversion rates among non-technical micro-business owners.

---

## 🗺️ Site Architecture Overview

Our structure is flat and highly focused, guiding users toward individual kit purchases or the high-margin **All-Access Bundle**.

```
tinyops.com/ (Homepage)
│
├── /kits (Product Catalog)
│   ├── /kits/client-onboarding (Flagship Kit)
│   ├── /kits/invoice-followup (Upcoming)
│   ├── /kits/review-collection (Upcoming)
│   ├── /kits/lead-response (Upcoming)
│   └── /kits/appointment-reminders (Upcoming)
│
├── /about (Brand Story & Philosophy)
└── /faq (Central Help & Objections)
```

## 📄 Detailed Page Specifications

### 1. Homepage (`/`)
*   **Goal:** Establish trust, explain the "AI workflow kit" category, showcase our flagship "Client Onboarding" kit, and upsell the All-Access Bundle.
*   **Target Audience:** Solopreneurs who feel overwhelmed by admin but are skeptical of complex, expensive SaaS platforms.
*   **Key Sections:**
    1.  **Hero Section:** Highly prominent headline stating value prop (e.g., "Automate your admin in 15 minutes. No coding. No monthly fees."). Bullet points on core benefits (10+ hours saved/wk, uses tools you already have). High-contrast CTA to browse kits.
    2.  **Social Proof / Trust Strip:** Logos of supported tools (Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Calendly, Stripe) + average customer rating badge.
    3.  **The Core Problem ("Before vs After"):** Contrast the painful manual workflow (chasing signatures, typing identical emails) with the automatic TinyOps flow.
    4.  **Flagship Product Feature:** In-depth preview of the "Client Onboarding Kit" ($67), showing what's inside and a prominent "Buy Kit" button.
    5.  **How It Works (3 Steps):**
        *   Step 1: Buy once (one-time payment, no subscription).
        *   Step 2: Copy-paste templates (Notion, Google Drive, Calendly).
        *   Step 3: Connect the "glue" (15-min Make.com automated setup guide).
    6.  **All-Access Bundle Upsell:** Prominent pricing card for the **All-Access Bundle** ($297 lifetime / $197 annual) showcasing high value (all current + future kits).
    7.  **Newsletter / Lead Magnet (Optional):** A free simple template in exchange for email (e.g., "Our 3-email cold-lead revival sequence").
    8.  **Footer:** Simple links, copyright, email support (`hello@tinyops.com`), and refund guarantee badge.

### 2. Product Catalog (`/kits`)
*   **Goal:** Showcase the breadth of our solutions and encourage multi-kit purchases or All-Access Bundle upgrades.
*   **Target Audience:** Customers browsing for specific pain points (e.g., "I need reviews" or "My invoice chasing is late").
*   **Key Sections:**
    1.  **Header:** "Ready-to-Install Automations for Every Admin Task."
    2.  **Filter Tabs:** All | Client Intake | Invoicing & Money | Reviews & Lead Prep.
    3.  **Kit Grid:**
        *   *Flagship:* **Client Onboarding Kit ($67)** - Active and ready.
        *   *Coming Soon:* **Invoice Follow-up Kit ($67)** - Automated late payment reminders.
        *   *Coming Soon:* **Review Collection Kit ($67)** - Post-project automatic Google Review requests.
        *   *Coming Soon:* **Lead Response Kit ($67)** - Fast, 5-minute automated reply to new website leads.
        *   *Coming Soon:* **Appointment Manager Kit ($67)** - Automated reminder and cancellation system.
    4.  **Footer Callout:** "Want all of them? Get the All-Access Bundle for $297."

### 3. Flagship Product Page (`/kits/client-onboarding`)
*   **Goal:** Single-product sales page designed for conversion. Must feel like a premium, highly actionable bundle.
*   **Target Audience:** Solopreneurs specifically seeking a client intake and proposal solution.
*   **Key Sections:**
    1.  **Hero Section:** High-contrast image of the Notion CRM and the Make.com flow. Price ($67) and "Buy Now" button.
    2.  **What You Get (The Deliverables Grid):**
        *   Notion Client CRM Template
        *   Google Forms Client Intake Questions
        *   Google Docs Proposal Template
        *   3-Email Welcome Sequence (Written & Ready)
        *   Make.com Automated blueprint & step-by-step setup guide
    3.  **Time Savings & ROI Calculator:** Show how the $67 purchase pays for itself in less than 2 hours of saved administrative work.
    4.  **Before vs After Chart:** Visual comparison of manual vs. automated steps.
    5.  **Interactive Walkthrough (Screencast Outline):** Placeholder description of the 5-minute setup video.
    6.  **Tool Stack Requirements:** Table showing Gmail, Notion, Calendly, Make.com (all free tiers are supported).
    7.  **Kit-Specific FAQ:** 5-6 tailored questions about client onboarding.
    8.  **Risk-Free Guarantee:** 30-day money-back guarantee seal.

### 4. About Page (`/about`)
*   **Goal:** Build strong relational trust by putting faces behind the brand.
*   **Target Audience:** Customers who buy from people and want to know who is supporting them if they get stuck.
*   **Key Sections:**
    1.  **The Problem We Faced:** Story of how we (as freelancers and product builders) spent 15 hours a week doing boring manual data entry instead of working with paying clients.
    2.  **Why We Built TinyOps:** We tried HoneyBook and Dubsado but found them expensive ($40/mo), complex, and annoying to migrate to. We wanted a system that utilized tools we already knew and loved (Notion, Google Docs) without monthly fees.
    3.  **Our Core Beliefs:**
        *   *No Subscription Fatigue:* Buy once, own forever.
        *   *15-Minute Rule:* If a kit takes more than 15 minutes to set up, it's too complicated.
        *   *Zero-Code:* Anyone who can copy-paste can run our systems.
    4.  **Who We Are:** Brief, friendly bio notes of the TinyOps team.

### 5. FAQ Page (`/faq`)
*   **Goal:** Standardize answers to pre-purchase objections to lower the customer support burden.
*   **Key Questions Answered:**
    *   *Do I need to pay for Notion, Calendly, or Make.com?* (No, the free tiers of these platforms are fully sufficient for our kits).
    *   *What if I'm not good with technology?* (We wrote our guides for complete beginners. If you can copy and paste, you can set this up).
    *   *Can I customize the emails and Notion database?* (Yes! They are completely yours to modify, rename, or color-code once copied).
    *   *What is your refund policy?* (We offer a 30-day, no-questions-asked refund if you're not completely satisfied with your time savings).
    *   *Can you install it for me?* (Yes, we offer a Done-for-You Setup Service for $247).
