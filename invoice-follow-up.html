Frequently Asked Questions

Answering your questions regarding late payment follow-ups.

Does this send reminders if the invoice has already been paid?

No. The Make.com automation performs an automatic real-time API filter check on your Google Sheets tracker. If the invoice row status has been updated to "Paid", the scenario exits immediately and never triggers subsequent reminder emails.

Do I need to pay for a premium Make.com account?

No. The Make.com free tier grants you 1,000 scenario operations per month. Since your invoice follow-up scenario only runs when an invoice is overdue (performing a few checks per day), it will use less than 150 operations per month, fitting comfortably in the free tier forever.

What payment tools are supported?

We support all major online billing platforms. The setup-guide provides exact integration steps for Stripe, PayPal Business, QuickBooks Online, and FreshBooks. If you use a different invoicing tool, you can easily use Google Sheets directly to track and run the automation manually.

Can I customize the email content?

Absolutely. The email templates are standard raw HTML/TXT files. You can open them, modify any wording, adjust spacing, add links to your calendar, or change the subject lines directly inside the Make.com Gmail nodes to fit your tone of voice.
